# cfKanban 1.9.0-rc.1

## 简体中文

相对正式版 `1.8.1`，本 RC 改善登录后的导航、长期使用的本地工作台和分组事项浏览。

- 已登录时，首页右上角提供进入工作台的入口，无需再次使用 Passkey。
- 完整版 WebUI 和本地事项列表按状态分组、独立分页，支持逐级展开子项；列表与看板显示可见直接子项的完成进度。
- 工作台在进程的八小时有效期内保留打开页面的授权，减少久置后导航失败，并保留明确关闭和过期边界。
- 语言和主题跟随账号偏好，详情布局与列表属性、分组和子项展开控件更清晰。
- 新建及恢复父子关系拒绝循环，支持多个父项；历史循环继续可读并在列表中截断、标注。

## English

Compared with stable `1.8.1`, this RC improves signed-in navigation, long-running local workbenches, and grouped Issue browsing.

- A signed-in homepage offers a workbench entry at the top right without another Passkey login.
- Full online and local lists group Issues by status with independent pagination and nested expansion; lists and boards show completion progress for visible direct children.
- Open workbench pages retain authorization within the process's eight-hour lifetime, reducing navigation failures after idle periods while preserving explicit close and expiry boundaries.
- Language and theme follow account preferences; Issue details and list property, group, and child controls are clearer.
- New and restored parent relations reject cycles and support multiple parents; historical cycles remain readable with truncation and warnings in lists.

## 升级与已知限制 / Upgrade and known limitations

从 1.8.1 升级需执行 `0019_principal_locale.sql`，新增可空语言偏好字段，schema 从 18 升到 19；API 版本仍为 0.1.0。升级前核对迁移校验账本、真实 schema 与 D1 恢复点。子项进度只统计有权限的直接子项，Canceled 不计为 Done；列表层级以当前已加载事项和有界父项摘要呈现。工作台进程超过八小时后需要重新打开。

Upgrading from 1.8.1 applies `0019_principal_locale.sql`, adding a nullable language preference and moving schema 18 to 19; the API version remains 0.1.0. Verify migration checksums, actual schema, and a D1 restore point before upgrading. Progress counts accessible direct children; Canceled is not Done. List hierarchies use loaded Issues and bounded parent summaries. Reopen the workbench after its eight-hour process lifetime.
