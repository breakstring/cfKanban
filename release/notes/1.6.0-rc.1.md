# cfKanban 1.6.0-rc.1 — 开发草稿 / Development draft

## 简体中文

相对已发布的 `1.5.0`，本开发候选增加 Owner 实例公告，支持网页登录会话按前台活动续期，并让活动历史更方便查阅。

- Owner 可以发布、撤回维护安排等公告；用户可选择是否接收，在网页或 Agent 中查看并逐条确认，历史保留过期和撤回状态，并减少历史增长后的重复数据库读取。
- 管理操作记录和项目活动最新在前，继续加载较早记录，刷新后查看新增活动；Agent 的原有增量读取保持兼容。
- 在网页前台操作时自动延长有效会话，每三十分钟最多续期一次、每次八小时、首次登录后最多七天；过期后可在当前页面复制草稿，重新登录后显式恢复，不自动提交。
- 后续发版说明采用简洁双语模板，先介绍用户和管理员的改善，再提供必要升级提醒与真实提交链接。

## English

Compared with the published `1.5.0`, this development candidate adds Owner announcements, renews browser sessions during foreground activity, and makes activity history easier to browse.

- Owners can publish or withdraw announcements such as maintenance plans. People choose reception, read and individually confirm notices through Web or Agent, and retain history with expired or withdrawn status. Recurring notice checks avoid repeatedly reading that growing history.
- Management and project activity show the newest records first, load older history on demand, and reveal new activity after refresh. Existing Agent incremental reads remain compatible.
- Foreground interactions renew valid browser sessions at most once every thirty minutes, for eight hours at a time and up to seven days from login. After expiry, copy drafts in the current page or explicitly restore them after signing in again; writes are never automatically resubmitted.
- Future release notes use a concise bilingual template, leading with user and administrator improvements, followed by necessary upgrade guidance and verified commit links.

## 升级与已知限制 / Upgrade and known limitations

新增三项兼容 migration，将 schema 14 升至 17；升级时先迁移再部署 Worker，Worker 回滚不回退 D1。实例升级与 Skill 更新分别执行。升级前的登录 Cookie 保留原八小时期限，到期后重新登录即可使用完整续期周期。草稿仅保留在当前页面内存，刷新或关闭页面会丢失；重新登录须仍为原 Principal 且具有相应权限。旧 Worker 不保证已续期会话的期限行为。通知在下次正常调用时发现，不实时唤醒 Agent；回复中断或确认失败可能再次提醒。操作方式见[通知手册](../../apps/docs/zh-CN/usage/notifications.md)和[升级指南](../../apps/docs/zh-CN/deployment/updates.md)。

Three compatible migrations move schema 14 to 17. Apply them before deploying the Worker; Worker rollback does not reverse D1 migrations. Instance upgrades and Skill updates are separate. Pre-upgrade sign-in cookies keep their original eight-hour deadline; sign in again after that deadline to use the full renewal period. Drafts remain in the current page memory and are lost on refresh or close; restoration requires the same Principal and current permissions. Older Workers do not guarantee expiry behavior for renewed sessions. Notices are discovered on the next ordinary call and do not wake an idle Agent; interruptions or failed confirmation may repeat reminders. See the [notification guide](../../apps/docs/en/usage/notifications.md) and [upgrade guide](../../apps/docs/en/deployment/updates.md).
