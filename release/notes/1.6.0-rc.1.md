# cfKanban 1.6.0-rc.1 — 开发草稿 / Development draft

## 简体中文

相对已发布的 `1.5.0`，本开发候选增加 Owner 实例公告，并让活动历史更方便查阅。

- Owner 可以发布、撤回维护安排等公告；用户可选择是否接收，在网页或 Agent 中查看并逐条确认，历史保留过期和撤回状态。
- 管理操作记录和项目活动最新在前，继续加载较早记录，刷新后查看新增活动；Agent 的原有增量读取保持兼容。
- 后续发版说明采用简洁双语模板，先介绍用户和管理员的改善，再提供必要升级提醒与真实提交链接。

## English

Compared with the published `1.5.0`, this development candidate adds Owner announcements and makes activity history easier to browse.

- Owners can publish or withdraw announcements such as maintenance plans. People choose reception, read and individually confirm notices through Web or Agent, and retain history with expired or withdrawn status.
- Management and project activity show the newest records first, load older history on demand, and reveal new activity after refresh. Existing Agent incremental reads remain compatible.
- Future release notes use a concise bilingual template, leading with user and administrator improvements, followed by necessary upgrade guidance and verified commit links.

## 升级与已知限制 / Upgrade and known limitations

新增两项兼容 migration，将 schema 14 升至 16；升级时先迁移再部署 Worker，Worker 回滚不回退 D1。实例升级与 Skill 更新分别执行。通知在下次正常调用时发现，不实时唤醒 Agent；回复中断或确认失败可能再次提醒。操作方式见[通知手册](../../apps/docs/zh-CN/usage/notifications.md)和[升级指南](../../apps/docs/zh-CN/deployment/updates.md)。

Two compatible migrations move schema 14 to 16. Apply them before deploying the Worker; Worker rollback does not reverse D1 migrations. Instance upgrades and Skill updates are separate. Notices are discovered on the next ordinary call and do not wake an idle Agent; interruptions or failed confirmation may repeat reminders. See the [notification guide](../../apps/docs/en/usage/notifications.md) and [upgrade guide](../../apps/docs/en/deployment/updates.md).
