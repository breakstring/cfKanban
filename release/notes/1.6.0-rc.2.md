# cfKanban 1.6.0-rc.2

## 简体中文

相对已公开的 `1.6.0-rc.1`，本 RC 修复真实浏览器中活动续期未生效的问题，恢复既定的网页登录行为。[CFK-529](https://cfkanban.dev/app/issues/CFK-529)

- 前台鼠标、键盘和触屏操作可正常触发续期，减少活跃使用时被登录到期打断。
- 持续编辑时，正常续期保持当前表单与未提交输入，无需重新加载业务页面。
- 到期前与多标签页的会话校验恢复正常调度；已续期的有效会话不会被旧到期计时误清，真正到期后仍需重新登录。

## English

Compared with the published `1.6.0-rc.1`, this RC fixes activity renewal failing in real browsers and restores the intended browser sign-in behavior. [CFK-529](https://cfkanban.dev/app/issues/CFK-529)

- Foreground mouse, keyboard, and touch interactions can renew sessions normally, reducing sign-in interruptions during active use.
- Normal renewal keeps the current editing form and unsaved input without reloading the business page.
- Checks before expiry and between tabs are scheduled normally again. An old expiry timer does not clear a valid renewed session; genuinely expired sessions still require signing in again.

## 升级与已知限制 / Upgrade and known limitations

从 `rc.1` 升级，schema **17 → 17**，无新增迁移，仍须核对实际 schema 与迁移记录。原有 **8 小时滑动期限、30 分钟续期间隔、首次登录后 7 天绝对上限**不变；后台请求不续期，续期不修改 Cookie 或扩大权限。旧 Cookie 仍按原 8 小时到期，重新登录后使用新规则。文字草稿仅在本页内存中保留，刷新或关闭页面会丢失。本 RC 用于先行验证，不代表已完成线上验收。[会话续期规则](https://github.com/breakstring/cfKanban/blob/__CANDIDATE_SHA__/docs/specs/2026-10-01-web-session-renewal-spec.md)

Upgrading from `rc.1` keeps schema at **17 → 17**, with no new migrations; verify the actual schema and migration ledger. The existing **8-hour sliding lifetime, 30-minute renewal interval, and 7-day absolute limit from initial sign-in** remain unchanged. Background requests do not renew sessions, and renewal does not modify cookies or broaden access. Older cookies retain their original 8-hour expiry; sign in again to obtain the new rules. Text drafts remain only in this page memory and are lost on refresh or close. This RC is for evaluation and does not claim completed live validation. [Session renewal rules](https://github.com/breakstring/cfKanban/blob/__CANDIDATE_SHA__/docs/specs/2026-10-01-web-session-renewal-spec.md)
